How to free up disk space without deleting what matters

Where disk space disappears on Windows and Mac, and what is safe to delete when the disk full warning appears.

First, find out where the space went

On Windows: Settings → System → Storage shows usage by category. On Mac: Apple menu → About This Mac → Storage → Manage. Almost always the culprits are: Downloads, old videos, a full Recycle Bin/Trash, and temporary files.

What is safe to delete

  • Recycle Bin / Trash: empty it. Anything in there is something you already decided to delete.
  • Temporary files older than a week (%temp% on Windows).
  • Installers for programs you have already installed.
  • Old downloads: sort by size and date.
  • Old Windows versions (the Windows.old folder), using the built-in Disk Cleanup tool.

What not to delete

Nothing inside C:\Windows, Program Files, or /System. Also don't delete folders with unfamiliar names inside Documents: they may belong to games or programs you use.
