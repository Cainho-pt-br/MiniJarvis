Internet slow or down? How to know who is to blame

A one-minute test to find out whether the problem is Wi-Fi, your router, your ISP, or your computer itself.

The one-minute test

  1. Another device: is your phone on the same Wi-Fi also slow? If so, the problem is not your computer.
  2. Near the router: walk closer to it. Did it improve? Then it's Wi-Fi range (walls, distance, microwave interference).
  3. Restart the router: unplug it, count to 30 seconds, plug it back in. This fixes half of all cases.
  4. Ping: on Windows, open Command Prompt and type ping 1.1.1.1. "Request timed out" means the internet is truly down; times above 100 ms indicate a poor connection.
  5. Still bad on a wired cable? Then it's your provider (ISP): call them with the ping result in hand.

If only your computer is slow

Check whether an update or program is downloading something in the background (Windows Update, games, cloud sync). In Task Manager, the Network column shows which app is using your connection.
