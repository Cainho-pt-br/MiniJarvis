Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what data Jarvis stores, why, and for how long, in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018). Data controller: {{RESPONSAVEL}}. Contact: {{CONTATO}}.

1. What we store

  • Account: name, email, password (only the hash, never the password itself), creation date, plan, and credit history.
  • Chats: the messages you exchange with Jarvis, so you can view your history and Jarvis can keep context.
  • Brain: the notes that you or Jarvis save.
  • Fleet: name, operating system, hardware, and resource usage (CPU, memory, disk) of the computers you connect, and the log of tasks executed on them, including command output.
  • Security: IP address and timestamp of signups, sign-in attempts, and verification codes (hash only) for up to 2 days; identifier of browsers where you verified your email.
  • Payments: identifier, amount, plan, and status of each payment. Card details stay with Mercado Pago; they never pass through our servers.

2. Who we share data with

  • Anthropic (USA): your messages, fleet status, and the notes Jarvis reads are sent to the Claude API to generate responses. Anthropic does not use API data to train models, according to its policy.
  • Vercel (hosting) and Neon (database, São Paulo region): where the Service runs and data is stored.
  • Mercado Pago: processes payments.
  • Email provider: sends verification codes and receipts.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Legal basis and purpose

We process data to perform our contract with you (provide the Service), comply with legal obligations (payment records), and for legitimate interest (security against abuse).

4. How long we keep data

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything is immediately deleted from our database, and any active subscription is canceled. Payment records remain with Mercado Pago for the period required by law.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, and delete your data directly in the panel (chats, notes, machines, and your entire account under Plans → Your account). To export your data or make any other request under the LGPD, write to {{CONTATO}}; we respond within 15 days.

6. Cookies

We use only two cookies, both strictly necessary: the sign-in session cookie and the trusted-browser identifier. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Security

Passwords hashed with PBKDF2, tokens stored only as hashes, HTTPS everywhere, rate limits against brute-force attempts, and full account deletion on request. No system is infallible: if an incident affects you, we will notify you.

8. Changes

If this policy changes significantly, we will notify you by email or in the panel before the change takes effect.
